4 years ago in 2017 I started this personal website and blog after going to a
personal branding workshop organised by the NUS School of Computing.

It turns out that writing well is really hard and it takes quite a lot of time,
so I stopped after writing a few articles in 2017.

Now, I realised the importance of developing the skill of writing well, and I'm
trying to work on it again! However, I do not feel ready enough to broadcast
my writings to the entire internet (it can be quite rough), so I'm making the
articles unlisted for the time being.

I am going to add a Telegram channel and/or a Twitter account that will track
my articles. Do subscribe to get updates! If you have previously subscribed
via RSS, do remove them as I plan to not use them anymore so that I can always
track at any given time who are following my writing.

I do have some thoughts that I really want to put into writing; hopefully I can
start the habit of writing again and not go silent after a few articles!
